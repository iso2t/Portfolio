# Heavy Inventories

Heavy Inventories adds weight to the things you carry. Packing for a trip means leaving room for what you bring back,
and a full inventory can make the journey home harder than the journey out.

These pages cover **4.263.0.0-rc.2 for Minecraft 26.3**, on Fabric and NeoForge. This is a release candidate. Older
releases may have different settings or dependencies.

## Players

Start with [installation](/heavy-inventories/installation), then [carrying weight](/heavy-inventories/carrying-weight). Find out what counts toward your load,
what happens when you carry too much, and how to increase your capacity.

The [player guide](/heavy-inventories/players) also covers enchantments, elytra flight, tooltips, and the weight display.

## Modpack makers

Use [server settings](/heavy-inventories/server-settings) to tune capacity and penalties, and [weight datapacks](/heavy-inventories/weight-datapacks) to
change individual item weights. Datapacks go on the server; players receive its weights when they join.

The [modpack guide](/heavy-inventories/modpack-makers) includes commands, weight reports, and importing older weight files.

## Developers

Start with the [developer guide](/heavy-inventories/developers) to integrate your mod. It covers plugin setup on both loaders, weight
queries, extra inventories, custom containers, capacity bonuses, HUD ownership, and update callbacks, with Java
examples. You can also bundle [weight definitions](/heavy-inventories/weight-datapacks) without writing a plugin.

## Something wrong?

Check [troubleshooting](/heavy-inventories/troubleshooting), or [report an issue](https://github.com/iso2t/Heavy-Inventories/issues) with
your Minecraft version, loader, mod version, and steps to reproduce it.
