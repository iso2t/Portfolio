# Elytra flight

You can deploy an elytra while overloaded. Carrying more weight reduces lift and rocket thrust, so leave some room in
your inventory before a long flight.

With default settings, the penalties grow from zero at no load to their maximum at **1,000 carried pounds**:

| Penalty               | Maximum |
|-----------------------|---------|
| Reduced lift          | 15%     |
| Reduced rocket thrust | 25%     |

They stop increasing above that weight. Rocket duration and normal elytra durability use are unchanged.

These percentages describe lift and thrust, not a fixed reduction in flight distance. Your height, pitch, speed, and use
of rockets still affect how far you travel.

## Carrying capacity doesn't change the flight penalty

Flight uses your actual carried weight. Strength, Bracing, and Reinforced can give you more room before becoming
encumbered, but a 1,000-pound load still has the same flight penalty.

Switching from a Bracing chestplate to an elytra also removes the chestplate's capacity bonus. Check your load before
taking off.

## Use Soaring to reduce the penalty

[Soaring](/heavy-inventories/enchantments) reduces the weight penalties by 20% per level, up to 80% at level IV. Unload items to reduce
them further.

Server owners can change the weight at which flight penalties reach their maximum, adjust each penalty, or disable them
in [server settings](/heavy-inventories/server-settings).
