# Sverve

Survival isn't hard enough.

Sverve adds thirst, heat, cold, wetness, and dry air to Minecraft survival,
using familiar items and mechanics to make the world harder to live in.

Drink water bottles to stay hydrated, prepare for the climate, and use heat
sources and protective enchantments to survive extreme conditions.

## Start here

- [Install Sverve](/sverve/installation) and its required dependencies.
- Read the [survival guide](/sverve/survival) before heading out.
- Adjust the [configuration](/sverve/configuration) for your server or modpack.

Sverve supports Fabric and NeoForge and requires [Easy Config](/easy-config).
It also has native integration with [Heavy Inventories](/heavy-inventories).
